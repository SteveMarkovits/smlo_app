Get in touch

The quickest way to reach us is the Send feedback option inside the app, under Settings. Bug reports, questions, and suggestions all go straight to us from there.

Prefer email? Write to us at touchgrasstechnologies@gmail.com.

Frequently asked

Is my data backed up? Yes — your tasks are stored on your device and backed up automatically whenever you have a connection, so reinstalling or switching devices won't lose your data.

How do I delete my data? Delete tasks individually from within the app, or uninstall the app to remove everything stored locally. To remove cloud-synced data as well, email us at the address above.
