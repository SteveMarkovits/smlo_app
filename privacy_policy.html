Privacy Policy for SMLO

Effective Date: 2025-07-11
Last Updated: 2025-08-31

Touch Grass Technologies ("we", "our", or "us") built the SMLO app ("Application") as a free app. This service is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your data in compliance with the UK GDPR and other applicable privacy laws. It applies to all platforms where SMLO is available, including Android and iOS.

1. Information We Collect

a. User-Generated Content

We collect information you provide when creating tasks in the app, such as:

  • Task descriptions
  • Dates/times
  • Notes

This data is stored locally on your device and, if syncing is enabled, backed up to a secure Google Firestore database.

Note: Task descriptions may contain personal information if you include names, birthdays, or other identifiers.

b. Device and Usage Data

We may collect limited technical and diagnostic data through third-party services, including:

  • Device type and OS version
  • Anonymous crash reports (if enabled)

We do not track users for advertising or behavioral profiling.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely to:

  • Operate and improve the app
  • Fix bugs and support functionality
  • Provide secure data backups

We do not sell or share your data with third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Our legal grounds under UK GDPR are:

  • Your consent
  • Legitimate interest (e.g., for bug fixing and syncing)

You can withdraw consent at any time by deleting your data or uninstalling the app.

4. Data Storage and Security

Data is securely stored using Google Firestore. Local data remains on your device unless you choose to sync. If you use iOS and enable iCloud backups, certain app data may also be stored securely by Apple under their terms and privacy policies.

Access to your data is limited to authorized support personnel only.

5. Data Retention and Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by:

  • Manually removing tasks
  • Uninstalling the app (removes local data)

To delete cloud-synced data, contact us at touchgrasstechnologies@gmail.com.

6. Data Transfers

Data may be processed on servers located outside the UK. Google complies with relevant international frameworks, including the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and Standard Contractual Clauses.

7. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access your data
  • Request correction or deletion
  • Object to processing
  • Port your data to another provider

To exercise these rights, contact us at touchgrasstechnologies@gmail.com.

8. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Google Play Services (Android version)
  • Apple Services (iOS version), including optional iCloud backups, crash diagnostics, and basic analytics provided by Apple

9. Children’s Privacy

SMLO is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we become aware of such data, we will delete it promptly.

10. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically. We recommend checking this page regularly for any changes.
